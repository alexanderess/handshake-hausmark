The Hausmark Renovation Consumer Confidence Score

About the RCCS

The RCCS is a decision-support tool designed to help homeowners evaluate and compare ID Firms in a structured and consistent way. Scores reflect the homeowner's observations and impressions at the time of assessment and should be used together with personal due diligence, contract review, budget considerations, and individual requirements. The RCCS is intended as a guide and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for any renovation decision.

Hausmark is an independent assessor of interior design firms and renovation contractors. We conduct real-world assessments through mystery shopping and on-site inspections, evaluating customer experience, professionalism, project management, transparency and more — so you can choose with confidence.

Our assessment process

How we assess ID firms.

  1. 1

    Select

    We shortlist ID firms based on homeowner feedback, public sentiment and industry nominations.

  2. 2

    Assess

    Our trained Hausmark Inspectors conduct mystery shopping and on-site visits, experiencing the ID process as real homeowners.

  3. 3

    Evaluate

    Firms are scored across multiple criteria — from design advice and quotation clarity to project management, workmanship and after-sales support.

  4. 4

    Moderate

    All findings are reviewed and moderated to ensure fairness, consistency and objectivity.

  5. 5

    Recognise

    Only ID firms that meet our standards earn the Hausmark Seal and are listed as Hausmark Accredited IDs.
