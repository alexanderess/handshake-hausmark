About the RCCS

The RCCS is a decision-support tool designed to help homeowners evaluate and compare ID Firms in a structured and consistent way. Scores reflect the homeowner's observations and impressions at the time of assessment and should be used together with personal due diligence, contract review, budget considerations, and individual requirements. The RCCS is intended as a guide and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for any renovation decision.

Pillar 9 · Escrow payment

Why the way you pay matters as much as who you hire.

Most renovation contracts in Singapore ask you to pay ahead of the work. A deposit on signing, then progress payments at each stage. Handshake's guidance is that no full home renovation needs a deposit above 20%, and that a fair schedule runs four to five stages with 10–15% upfront. Plenty of firms still ask for 30%, 40% or 50%.

With Handshake, your money does not go to your designer. You pay into a DBS escrow account instead, stage by stage. Money is only released at each stage of your renovation when the work is done and you approve it.

Handshake Renovation Safeguard

Handshake keeps your money safe at every stage, under three rules.

  • Deposit 100% protected

    No one can access your funds. Neither Handshake nor your interior designer has access — the money is held with DBS.

  • 50% of every payment protected

    You are in control. Each payment is held in escrow until that stage is complete and you approve it.

  • Full visibility

    You always know where your money is. Every payment, approval and release is visible to you at any time.

Payments run through a MAS-regulated escrow account at DBS. If you disagree about a stage, the balance stays where it is and neither side can move it alone, while Handshake runs a dispute process covering negotiation, mediation and arbitration. Should it reach CASE, the Small Claims Tribunal or court, your money is still in the account, and every payment and approval is on record.

How a milestone payment moves

Your ID invites you to the project

Every milestone and payment stage is set out and approved by you before any money moves.

You pay that stage into escrow

Into a DBS escrow account, not your designer's. Neither they nor Handshake can reach it.

Your ID completes the stage

Hacking, plumbing and electrical, carpentry — whatever the milestone covers. Drawings, invoices and updates stay in one shared channel.

You inspect and approve

Only then is that milestone released. Everything else stays held.

A dispute freezes the money, not the project

Neither side can move the funds alone. Negotiation, mediation or arbitration follows, on timelines agreed up front.
